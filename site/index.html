The shift

A company used to be its people.

Now people move up to strategy, taste and judgment — and a fleet of agents does the labor. What's left holding the company together isn't a headcount. It's the context everyone works from.

The old company

Knowledge trapped in people.

Every task waited on a calendar — a brief, emails, meetings, specialists, handoffs, revisions. The company moved only as fast as its busiest person, and the knowledge walked out the door each evening.

  • → brief, then meetings
  • → a specialist for each step
  • → handoffs & revisions
  • → weeks, and a big invoice

The AI-native company

Knowledge in a shared context layer.

The brief, the brand, the conventions, the playbooks, the decision logs — written down so well that agents can run the work. Humans set direction and keep taste; the fleet executes, in parallel, around the clock.

  • ◆ humans own strategy & taste
  • ◆ a legible, shared context layer
  • ◆ a fleet of specialist agents
  • ◆ review, then ship — in an afternoon

How it works

One orchestrator. The whole pipeline.

Brief → design → build → deploy → grow → monitor. Specialist agents work in parallel and hand off to each other — and you check in at the moments that matter.

01

Brief

You describe what to ship. The orchestrator turns it into scope and acceptance tests, and writes it into the context layer.

02

Design & build

Specialist agents work in parallel — a real design system on the canvas, then code, payments, and audits, reviewed by you.

03

Deploy

Shipped live to your infrastructure. Everything runs locally on your machine — your code and keys never leave it.

04

Grow & monitor

SEO, campaigns and a marketing film — then 24/7 monitoring with alerts on uptime, sales, traffic and security.

You stay in the loop.Approve the design, sign off the audit, hit go on launch. The fleet does the rest — and every run makes the context layer sharper.
